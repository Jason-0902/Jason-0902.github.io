Autoware Fuzzing for Autonomous Driving Systems

Undergraduate Research Project · Chung Yuan Christian University · December 2023 – April 2024

Research Advisor: Dr. Hsiao-Rong Tyan

Overview

An exploratory undergraduate project on whether fuzz testing could be applied to Autoware, an open-source autonomous driving platform built on ROS 2. The work was carried out as part of a student research team.

What I worked on

  • Reviewed how parts of Autoware could be tested with fuzzing tools.
  • Ran small fuzzing experiments with libFuzzer and AddressSanitizer.
  • Helped with the team's AFL++ and DriveFuzz experiments.

Tools

  • C++
  • Python
  • ROS 2
  • libFuzzer
  • AddressSanitizer
  • AFL++
  • DriveFuzz

Outcome

The project was a learning exercise in setting up and evaluating fuzzing workflows. It was an exploratory study and does not claim new vulnerability findings.

Award

Honorable Mention

Capstone Project Competition, Software and Network Category, Chung Yuan Christian University, April 2024.
